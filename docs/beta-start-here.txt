Dungeon World AI - Beta
Your world. Your table.

1. Open Dungeon-World-AI-0.2.2-beta.1-Windows-Setup.msi and choose Install.
2. Choose Finish to open local AI setup. Leave Local AI Dungeon Master selected.
3. Choose Set up AI. When downloads finish, choose Finish to open the app.

Upgrading from the earlier beta: save your work and close the app and its setup
window before running this installer. Keep the defaults. Select AI image
generation in local AI setup to retry the optional art installation. Existing
campaigns and model downloads are kept; uninstalling first is unnecessary.

An internet connection is needed for the initial AI downloads and for websites
you choose to open in Scrying Pane. Your Dungeon Master and generated artwork
run on your PC after setup. No AI account, subscription, or API key is needed.

AI image generation is optional and can be added later. Use Set up local AI
inside the app or in the Windows Start menu. NVIDIA graphics are used when
supported; CPU image generation can be slow. Narration uses the CPU in this beta.

Create a campaign, add a character, and explore the tabs. Changes save locally.
The first Dungeon Master reply or image can take longer while its model loads.

If a download stops, choose Retry. If you skip AI, campaign tools, maps, imported
artwork, dice, timers, and the browser remain available.

Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps > Dungeon World AI Beta. Your saved
campaigns, imported artwork, and downloaded models are kept for reinstalling.

Beta saves: %LOCALAPPDATA%\DungeonWorldAI\Beta\saved-games
Setup log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\DungeonWorldAI\Beta\setup.log

This beta installer is unsigned. Use the copy provided directly by the project.
If Windows blocks it, contact the beta organizer; do not disable your security
settings. Production distribution needs a signed installer.

Project, source code, known issues, and feedback:
https://github.com/ThisFriendJosh/Dungeon-World-AI

When reporting an issue, include what you clicked, what happened, and what you
expected. Screenshots and a Windows/GPU description are helpful. Share only
campaign information you want the project to see.
